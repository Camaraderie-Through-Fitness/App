Privacy Policy & Community Rules

Effective October 26, 2026

This page explains what the CTF community app (app.camaraderietf.org and the CTF app in app stores) collects, who can see it, and the rules everyone agrees to when they use it. The app is run by Camaraderie Through Fitness, Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit in Gainesville, Florida ("CTF," "we").

The short version · What we collect · Who can see what · How we use it · Services we use · Deleting your data · Community rules · Contact

The short version

  • We collect what you give us to run the community: your profile, your posts, your RSVPs, and your contact email.
  • We don't sell your information, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • Your full profile is only for you, your battle buddies, and CTF admins.
  • You can delete your account and your data from inside the app at any time.
  • There's zero tolerance for harassment, hate, threats, or explicit content. You can report posts and block people.

What we collect

Your account

Your email address and name. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with us. If you use email and a password, the password is handled by Google Firebase and CTF never sees it.

Your profile

Your name, photo (optional), and whether you're a veteran, active duty, Guard or Reserve, military family, or supporter, plus your branch. You can also add optional details: rank, job specialty, unit, years served, a short bio, how to reach you, and social media links.

What you post

Fox Hole posts and photos, comments, salutes, and any past posts you stash in your cache.

Events and connections

Events you RSVP to, your reminder email setting, and your battle buddy connections.

Gym reimbursement (only if you apply)

Your gym, membership type, join date, gym member number, the amount you paid, a photo of your receipt, and the Venmo or Cash App username where you want to be paid.

Things CTF admins record

Badges admins give after checking in person, such as Verified veteran, Hooks of Hope alum, or Atlas Clinics participant. Records of memberships or awards CTF paid for.

Activity

The last time you opened the app. We use this to see who's active and who we haven't heard from in a while.

Reports and blocks

Reports you send about posts or comments (including a copy of what was reported), and the list of people you've blocked.

Your agreement

The date you agreed to this policy and the community rules.

Stored only on your phone

A few settings stay on your device and are never sent to us: night mode, the last page you were on, and similar conveniences.

We don't collect your location, your contacts, health data from your phone, or advertising identifiers.

How we use it

  • To run the community: profiles, the member list, the Fox Hole, battle buddies, and events.
  • To email you a reminder the day before an event you RSVP'd to, if you turned reminders on. You can turn them off in your profile.
  • To review and pay gym reimbursements.
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reports, removing content that breaks the rules, and removing accounts when needed.
  • To contact you about your account or if there's a security problem.

We don't sell, rent, or trade your information, and we don't share it with anyone for their marketing. We'll only share it beyond what's described here if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Services we use

These companies help run the app. They handle data only as needed to provide their service.

  • Google Firebase stores your account and the app's data, and handles sign-in.
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Google Apps Script and Gmail send event reminder emails from CTF's Google account.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app's files. Like any web host, it may log basic technical details such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts supplies the app's fonts, which means Google can see your IP address when the fonts load.

Some links open other services that have their own privacy policies: the donation page, Zoom calls, maps, partner websites, and Venmo or Cash App when CTF pays a reimbursement. If you got the app from the Apple App Store or Google Play, Apple or Google also handle the download under their own policies.

How we protect it

Database security rules limit who can read each piece of information, as described in the table above. No system is perfect. If we learn that your information was exposed without permission, we'll tell you by email as the law requires.

Deleting your data

Open your account menu in the app and tap Delete my account. That permanently removes:

  • Your profile and profile details, plus your email and reminder setting
  • Your RSVPs
  • Your Fox Hole post and photo, your cache, and your comments and salutes
  • Your battle buddy connections, block list, and last-active time
  • Any reimbursement request that hasn't been approved yet
  • Your sign-in account

Some records stay after you delete your account:

  • Records of reimbursements CTF approved or paid stay in our financial records.
  • Reports you sent stay until an admin resolves them.

You can also edit or remove most information yourself at any time, or email us to ask for a copy of your data or to have it corrected or deleted.

Age

The CTF app is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we'll delete it.

Community rules

CTF exists so veterans and the people who support them can lean on each other. Speak freely and treat each other with respect. By using the app, you agree to these rules.

Not allowed

  • Harassing, bullying, or intimidating anyone
  • Threats of violence, or encouraging someone to hurt themselves or others
  • Hate speech or slurs targeting race, ethnicity, religion, sex, sexual orientation, disability, or any group
  • Sexual, pornographic, or graphically violent content
  • Sharing someone else's private information or photos without their permission
  • Spam, scams, or selling things
  • Pretending to be someone else, or falsely claiming military service
  • Anything illegal
Zero tolerance. There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Content that breaks these rules will be removed, and people who break them can lose access to the app.

Reporting and blocking

  • Tap ⋯ on any post or comment to report it or to block the person who wrote it.
  • When you block someone, you stop seeing their posts, comments, and profile, and any battle buddy connection between you ends. They aren't told.
  • CTF admins review reports within 24 hours. They remove content that breaks the rules and remove accounts of people who are abusive.
  • Photos are reviewed by an admin before anyone else sees them.
If someone is in danger, don't wait on a report. Call 911, or call the Veterans Crisis Line at 988 and press 1, or text 838255.

Changes to this page

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, the app will ask you to review and agree again.
