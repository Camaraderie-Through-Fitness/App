App Support

Need help with the CTF app? Email CTF@camaraderietf.org and a CTF volunteer will get back to you, usually within a few days.

In crisis right now? Call the Veterans Crisis Line at 988 and press 1, or text 838255. Free and confidential, 24/7. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

Common questions

How do I sign in?

The easiest way is Continue with Google. You can also create an account with your email and a password.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap Forgot password? We'll email you a reset link. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

I made my account with Google but want to use a password

Enter your Gmail address on the sign-in screen and tap Forgot password? to set one.

How do I change my profile or who can see it?

Tap your picture at the top right, then Edit profile. You can hide yourself from the member list there. Your full profile is only visible to you, your battle buddies, and CTF admins.

How do I report a post or block someone?

Tap ⋯ on any post or comment, then choose Report or Block. CTF admins review reports within 24 hours. You can unblock people from your account menu.

How do I turn off event reminder emails?

Tap your picture, then Edit profile, and turn off the reminder setting.

How do I delete my account?

Tap your picture at the top right, then Delete my account at the bottom of the menu. This permanently removes your profile and your posts. See our privacy policy for details.
